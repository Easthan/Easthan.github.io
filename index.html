What I do

Acoustic writing, electronic tools, music for picture and play.

  1. 01 Composition Chamber and concert music. Two works performed on UTK’s New Sound concert series, including a piano trio. →
  2. 02 Electronic Music Production, sound design, and live electronics in Ableton Live, on my own and with the UTK Electroacoustic Ensemble. →
  3. 03 Film Scoring Writing to picture: re-scoring film scenes to shape pacing, tension, and character. →
  4. 04 Game Audio Music and sound for interactive scenes, built and implemented in Unreal Engine. →
  5. 05 Piano Pianist and keyboardist, as comfortable at a grand piano as at a MIDI controller. →

About

Composer, pianist, and producer based in Knoxville.

I’m studying composition at the University of Tennessee, where my work moves between chamber music, electronic production, and scoring for film and games. I perform with the UTK Electroacoustic Ensemble and spend most of my studio time in Ableton Live.
